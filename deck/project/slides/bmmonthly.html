This is our own analysis of Elexon’s settlement data: every bid and offer NESO accepted in the Balancing Mechanism, month by month since January 2023. For most of that period NESO paid out between roughly £100 and £250 million a month. Then look at the right-hand end. September 2026 was £508 million and October £681 million, the highest month in the dataset. From January to October this year NESO has paid out £3.15 billion through the BM, which is 62% more than the same ten months of 2025 and already more than the whole of last year. It isn’t only prices: the volume of offers NESO accepted in October was about half as much again as a year earlier. Two caveats: this is indicative settlement data, which Elexon can still revise, and it’s Balancing Mechanism payments only, so it excludes trades, reserve and other service contracts that make up the rest of NESO’s balancing bill. NESO’s own monthly reports for September and October will tell us how much of this is constraints, but the direction is unmistakable.
