Flexibility & Demand Side Response · November 2026

The exploding cost
of balancing the grid

Why NESO is struggling, how it plans to fix it, and why our customers’ flexibility is part of the answer

Drawing on NESO, DESNZ and Elexon data, including our own analysis of Balancing Mechanism payments

Welcome. This talk is about money, and specifically the rapidly rising cost of keeping the GB grid balanced. I’ll cover five things: how fast balancing costs are growing; what NESO actually does to balance the system and why it has to; the difficulties it faces and where they come from; what NESO and government are doing to fix them; and finally why demand side response from our industrial and commercial customers is a direct answer to these problems, and an opportunity for them to earn from flexibility they already have. The Balancing Mechanism charts are our own analysis of Elexon’s public settlement data; everything else is sourced on the slides.
