Flexibility & Demand Side Response · November 2026

Balancing the grid:
rising costs and the
role of flexibility

Why NESO is struggling, how it plans to fix it, and why our customers’ flexibility is part of the answer

Data: NESO Data Portal, Elexon BMRS Insights Solution and DESNZ publications

Welcome. This talk is about money, and specifically the rapidly rising cost of keeping the GB grid balanced. I’ll cover five things: how fast balancing costs are growing; what NESO actually does to balance the system and why it has to; the difficulties it faces and where they come from; what NESO and government are doing to fix them; and finally why demand side response from our industrial and commercial customers is a direct answer to these problems, and an opportunity for them to earn from flexibility they already have. The Balancing Mechanism figures are built from Elexon’s public BMRS settlement data, and every slide carries its sources in the footer.
