05 · Flexibility

Every fix on the table makes flexible demand more visible, more valuable and better paid.

Which brings me to the main point. Look back at the list of difficulties: constraints, no locational signal, assets NESO can’t see, coarse settlement. Every one of the reforms makes flexible demand more useful: lower thresholds and visibility requirements bring it into NESO’s view, 15-minute settlement rewards fast and accurate response, locational procurement and the levy trial reward being in the right place. And NESO is clear that congestion will remain, so it will keep needing someone to soak up or step back. Our industrial and commercial customers are exactly that someone.
