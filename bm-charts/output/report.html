Source: Elexon BMRS Insights API, indicative settlement cashflows and volumes.

  • Offers: NESO pays a unit to increase output or reduce demand.
  • Bids: a unit reduces output or increases demand. Some are paid by NESO to do so (e.g. wind farms behind a constraint); others pay NESO back (e.g. gas plant saving fuel). The charts show only money paid out.
  • These are Balancing Mechanism costs from Elexon's indicative settlement data, not NESO's total balancing costs, which also include trades outside the BM and ancillary services.
