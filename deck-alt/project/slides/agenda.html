Five parts. First the cost: how much NESO spends balancing the system and how sharply that has risen, including a big jump this autumn. Second, NESO’s role: why a system operator has to balance at all, and how it does it, mainly through the Balancing Mechanism. Third, the difficulties: network constraints, a wholesale market with a single national price that ignores location, and a growing fleet of distributed assets NESO can’t see. Fourth, what’s being done: government rejected zonal pricing, so NESO is working through reformed national pricing and a set of balancing reforms. And finally, flexibility: how demand side response from our industrial and commercial customers tackles each of these, and what they can earn.
